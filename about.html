We Are Feane

Founded in 2010, Feane started as a small burger joint with a big dream — to serve the most delicious, freshest fast food in the city. Over the years, we've grown into a beloved restaurant destination, but our core values remain unchanged.

We believe that fast food doesn't have to mean low quality. Every ingredient we use is sourced fresh daily. Our chefs craft each dish with care, passion, and a commitment to flavor that you can taste in every single bite.

From our signature burgers to handcrafted pizzas and creamy pastas, Feane is where great taste meets great company.
